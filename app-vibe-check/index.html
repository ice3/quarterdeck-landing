KANDO

App preview for iPhone.

A clickable sketch of the first version, to agree on the screens before writing native code. Everything you see is tappable.

  1. Getting in. Request access with a work email, or use an invitation from a member.
  2. Home. Posts from people in yachting, plus the show card when a yacht show is on.
  3. Rooms. Groups by invitation or by request, for a profession or a club.
  4. Roundtables. One topic, a few days, everyone gives a view. Not a video call.
  5. Messages and profile. Each connection remembers where you met.

People, companies, vessels and rooms are fictional. Interface text is temporary; the final words come later.
